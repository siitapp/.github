## What is Siit?

Siit is an **AI-first ITSM platform**: an internal service desk for **IT, HR, and Finance** teams.

Employees ask for help in plain language, and Siit's AI agents triage the request, answer from your company knowledge, and resolve it autonomously when they can, handing off to a human only when needed. The result: faster answers for employees, less repetitive work for internal teams.

## How it works

- **🤖 AI agents:** Understand, triage, and resolve employee requests automatically, drawing on your knowledge base and connected systems.
- **💬 Meet employees where they are:** Requests come in through **Slack, Microsoft Teams, email, and a self-service portal**. No new tool to learn.
- **⚙️ Workflows:** Deterministic, rule-based automation (trigger → conditions → actions) for the processes you want to run exactly the same way every time.
- **📚 Knowledge:** Self-service answers and AI assistance powered by your articles and connected knowledge sources.
- **💻 Equipment & access:** Track assets and manage app access and provisioning across your stack.
- **🔗 Unified data:** Bring together identity and people data from your HRIS, MDM, and IAM tools so requests are handled with the right context.

## Who it's for

Internal **IT, HR, and Finance** teams at growing and enterprise companies who want to deliver a great employee experience while scaling support without scaling headcount.

## For developers

Build on top of Siit with our developer platform at **[developer.siit.io](https://developer.siit.io)**:

- **REST API:** Manage requests, users, teams & departments, applications, services & equipment, articles, approvals, workflows, and tags programmatically.
- **MCP server:** Connect Siit to any MCP-compatible AI client or agent at `https://mcp.siit.io/mcp`. See the [MCP reference](https://developer.siit.io/api-reference/mcp).
- **Webhooks & automation:** Drive your own workflows from Siit events.
